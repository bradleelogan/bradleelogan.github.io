About

Hi! My name is Bradlee Logan, and I am a cinematographer, editor, game designer, and more. I have a passion for learning new skills and applying what I learn to other aspects in life.

I like to challenge the statement of "jack of all trades, master of none" by showcasing how my expansive set of skills improve upon each other. By being an editor, I know how to get those shots that make the editing room thank me. By being an actor, I know how to give direction. By being the sound guy, I know how to pick up the role that many people seem to avoid. I strive to redefine the negative stigma often given to people who identify as a jack-of-all trades and help people realize that full modern saying: "jack_of_all trades, maser of none, but oftentimes better than a master of one."

I enjoy the work I do, but when I am not working, I am usually playing video games with friends
