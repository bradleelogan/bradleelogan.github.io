About

Hi! My name is Bradlee Logan, and I am a cinematographer, editor, game designer, and more. I graduated from the University of South Carolina with a Bachelor of Arts in Media Arts and a minor in Computer Science. I really enjoy the entire filmmaking process, but I particularly like the editing side.

Since around 2019, I've been drawn to editing. I took classes in high school that would use Premiere Pro, and in order to keep editing at home, I downloaded DaVinci Resolve to work on personal projects. I would continue to edit throughout my academic career despite being on track to major in Computer Science. My major didn't switch until 2024, after I found my passion by creating a fan-made music video for Good Kid's “Bubbly.” It received high praise at 1080c's (a club at USC) film festival, Media Fest 2024. After the amount of feedback I received, I was compelled to switch my major and pursue my love for creating things that people enjoy.

While DaVinci is my preferred editing software, I am well-versed in Premiere and other editing software. I am also proficient in multiple coding languages, including HTML, CSS, Python, Java, and C#. I built on these skills even more to create this website to display all of my work. In terms of the film roles I perform in, I mostly have experience in editing, acting, lighting, sound, and camera work. If a project requires a skill that I don't have, I will quickly research and learn it to get the job done.

I have a passion for learning new skills and applying what I learn to other areas in life. I like to challenge the statement of "jack-of-all-trades, master of none" by showcasing how my expansive set of skills complement each other. By being an editor, I know how to get those shots that make the editing room thank me. By being an actor, I know how to give direction. By being the sound guy, I know how to pick up the role that many people seem to avoid. I strive to redefine the negative stigma often given to people who identify as a jack-of-all-trades and help people realize that full modern saying: "jack-of-all-trades, master of none, but oftentimes better than a master of one.”

I enjoy and take pride in all the work I do, but when I'm not working, I'm usually playing video games with my friends or spending time with my partner.
